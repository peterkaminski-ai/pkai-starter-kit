# The bookshelf

A small shelf of books that comes with your agent. Each folder is one book; each page is a chapter; the chapters stand alone, so read in any order. Your agent can read them for itself, walk through one with you, or bring a chapter up when the moment calls for it.

The books were wikis in earlier versions of this kit (V1 and V2 called the shelf `pkai-starter/`). They're books now because that's what they are: a lesson, printed. The live version of any lesson — a workshop, a jam, a sitting with your agent — is always better than the book; the book is the standard way to carry the lesson from one house to another.

## Books and their boxes

A finished book is the polished face of a working pile: drafts, correspondence, transcripts, the arguments that didn't make it in. An archive keeps them side by side — the published book on the shelf, the boxes of letters that made it one level down. This shelf does the same: where a book has a `sources/` folder, that's its boxes. Read the book; go to the boxes when you want to know why.

## Whose words

*Principal* is the person an agent belongs to and works for. *House* is the whole you build and live in: an agent home plus a headquarters. *Estate* is what a house grows into when it holds more than one agent and a body of knowledge the agents keep and find their way around. *Room* is one working space: a call, a pad, a channel. A *pad* is a shared document, on MeetingWords or its like, that several people and agents write in at once. A *jam* is a session where more than one house meets in a room, each person with their agent. The books use one word per meaning and try not to use two words for one thing in the same chapter.
