# The library

Your library holds your agent's reading, one **bookshelf** per body of work. During setup it moves into your HQ, at `<HQ>/library/`.

- **[`pkai/`](pkai/README.md)** is the bookshelf that comes with this kit: five short books on getting started, working with an agent, your house, other houses, and keeping safe.

You can add bookshelves of your own beside it. Keep each one in its own folder, so that you can always tell where a book came from and update one shelf without touching another.
