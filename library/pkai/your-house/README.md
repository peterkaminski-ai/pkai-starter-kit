# A small house or a large one

How a house grows, and the ways into it. This book is for the moment one agent stops being enough — or for the person who arrives already knowing they'll want more than one — and for the person who meets their agent through a phone.

A **house** is an agent home and a headquarters, together. A **larger house** is what a house becomes when it holds several agents, some in the foreground and some in the background, and a body of knowledge those agents keep and find their way around. Nothing is torn down to get from one to the other; the shape this kit builds is the small end of the same shape.
