# Claude Desktop app: tips, workarounds and gotchas

*Version 1.0 · 2026-11-10 · collecting for v4.1*

This page collects what people learn using the **Code tab of the Claude Desktop app** with their agents: shortcuts, workarounds, and things that surprised them. v4.1, planned for December, will turn it into proper documentation. If you have an agent from v2 or v3, this is the most useful thing you can help with: use the Desktop app with your agent sometimes, then more and more, and tell us what you find.

Add to it: [open an issue](https://github.com/peterkaminski-ai/pkai-starter-kit/issues), open a pull request, or email kaminski@istori.com or freya@istori.com. Say what you did, what you expected, what actually happened, and whether it was on a Mac or Windows.

## Getting the app
- **The download page is claude.ai/download.** If you're signed in on claude.ai, the download is also behind the icon next to your name: **Get apps and extensions**. The "Desktop" and "Claude Code" download buttons give the same app.
- **The Desktop app asks you to sign in on its own,** even if you're already signed in on the web.
- **Using two computers at once?** If a sign-in code screen won't go away, **Sign in here instead** clears it.

## A brand-new Mac
- **Spotlight and the Apps view can be empty for a while** on a new Mac, while it indexes. Finder → Applications (Shift-Command-A) always works.
- **First launch** asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click **Open**.
- **In a folder picker,** Shift-Command-H jumps to your home folder.

## Sessions and folders
- **The sidebar groups sessions by folder name,** not by environment name.
- **Rename a session:** hover over it, click the ⋯ menu, and choose Rename. New sessions start out called "New Session".
- **Archive, don't delete:** hover over the session → ⋯ → **Archive**.
- **Environments:** New → Choose Environment → + New Environment → Local. The form may open already filled in with a temporary environment. Rename it and set its folders.

## Installers that hide
- **Apple's developer-tools window can open behind the app.** If you're waiting on it, click the blue installer icon in the Dock to bring it forward. Its time estimate jumps around; leave it running.

## Questions and answers in the Code tab
- **You can type your own answer** to a multiple-choice question; you don't have to pick an option first.
